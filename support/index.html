Welcome

Thank you for playing Sketch Escape. If you have a question or run into a problem, try the steps below or contact us directly.

Your game data

Sketch Escape does not require an account or login. Your game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Please note: Local progress may not transfer to another device. Removing and reinstalling the app may also erase your progress and saved preferences.

Basic troubleshooting

  1. Close Sketch Escape completely, then open the app again.
  2. Restart your iPhone or iPad.
  3. Check that your device is running the latest available version of iOS or iPadOS.
  4. Reinstall the app only if the other steps do not help. Reinstalling may permanently remove locally stored progress and preferences.

Contact support
